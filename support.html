Stampvale

Photo labels, made deliberate.

Get a stamped copy

Choose a photo. Select capture date, a manual date, or no date. Add a caption and optionally type a place. Preview the label, then export a separate copy. Share it or save the completed copy to Photos. Your original remains unchanged.

Why is capture date unavailable?

The photo may not have valid EXIF capture metadata. Screenshots and edited files often do not. Choose a manual calendar date or no date; the app does not silently replace missing metadata.

Dates and timezones

The date is the calendar date written in the photo, without conversion to your device's timezone. A missing timezone is shown as unknown. Dates are editable labels, not verified evidence of an event.

Image and batch limits

JPEG, HEIC and PNG up to 24 megapixels and 128 MB each. Lifetime Pro adds up to 40 photos per batch and 12 caption/style presets. Imports and completed working copies each have a 1 GB limit. Export a smaller batch if a limit is reached. Partial or cancelled exports clearly report completed copies.

Restore Lifetime Pro

Open Lifetime Pro and choose Restore purchases using the same Apple Account. Pending approval does not unlock Pro until Apple verifies the purchase. Single-photo export stays free.

Saving and sharing

Grant add-only Photos permission when saving copies. If denied, share to Files or enable Photos access in Settings. Keep Stampvale open while exporting. Completed temporary copies clear on the next app launch or selection change; save them to a destination you choose.
