Stampvale

Your photos stay on your device.

Privacy policy · effective October 16, 2026

Stampvale processes selected photos on your device. No account, advertising, tracking SDK, analytics upload, AI service or product backend. It does not request camera or location access. Apple's photo picker shares only photos you choose; Apple may download cloud-backed items.

Photo labels and copies

Dates are editable labels. Capture metadata may be wrong, lack a timezone, or be absent. Stampvale never silently substitutes the current time or file modification date. Optional location is text you type, off by default.

Exported PNG copies contain your visible labels and no copied source metadata, including GPS. Original library photos are not edited. Review labels before sharing. Recipients and destinations control copies after sharing or saving.

Local storage

Working photos and exports stay in a private temporary workspace until you change photos or next launch the app. Caption/style presets, preferences and content-free event counters stay on your device until removed or app deletion. Preferences may be included in system backups under your Apple settings. Presets can be deleted in the app.

Purchases

Apple handles purchases. Stampvale reads verified entitlement status and does not receive your payment card details.
